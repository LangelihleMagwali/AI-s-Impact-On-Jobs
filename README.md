# AI's Impact on Jobs: A Reproducible Research Project

## Project Title

**The Impact of AI Adoption on Job Growth and Automation Risk: A Data-Driven Analysis**

## Authors

- Langelihle Magwali
- Chiedza H. Chimedza

## Project Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming increasingly important in the modern labour market. It is changing the way people work, the skills employers require, and the types of jobs that are likely to grow or decline in the future.

This project investigates how AI adoption is related to job growth and automation risk. Using a job market dataset, we explore whether jobs with higher AI adoption are more likely to grow, whether they face higher automation risk, and which skills are most common in jobs that are growing and less likely to be automated.

The project follows a reproducible research structure, meaning that the data, code, outputs, and analysis are organised so that another person can run the project and reproduce the results.

## Research Questions

This project is guided by the following research questions:

1. How does AI adoption affect job growth?
2. How is AI adoption related to automation risk?
3. Which skills are associated with high-growth and low-automation-risk jobs?
4. Which industries show the highest levels of job growth?
5. Which job categories appear to be most affected by AI adoption?

## Dataset Description

The dataset used in this project is a synthetic job market dataset containing information about job listings and their relationship with AI adoption.

The dataset includes variables such as:

- Job title
- Industry
- Company size
- AI adoption level
- Automation risk
- Required skills
- Salary information
- Remote work ratio
- Job growth projection
